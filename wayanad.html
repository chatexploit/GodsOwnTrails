Wayanad

Wayanad is a lush hill region known for its mist-covered mountains, expansive forests, and peaceful rural landscapes. Waterfalls, wildlife sanctuaries, and spice plantations create a serene atmosphere where nature dominates every corner. The cool climate, scenic trekking trails, and stretches of tea and coffee estates make Wayanad an ideal getaway for travelers seeking tranquility, greenery, and a deeper connection with the natural world. Its blend of untouched wilderness and warm village life offers a refreshing escape from busy city routines..
